# Snap & Pop

Current product authority: `Snap_Pop_UI_MASTER_LOGIC_REV_12.md`

Snap & Pop is the connected learning system's **thought-to-expression specialist**.

Primary tagline:

**아이디어를 Snap! 이야기로 Pop!**

## Current role

Snap & Pop supports:
- Korean writing / idea development
- English sentence writing
- learned-word sentence use
- English speaking / short oral expression
- sentence expansion and revision

AI/Guide must not replace the child's authorship of the final thought, sentence, speech or writing.

`session_id / goal_id / task_id / lap_id / return_target`
